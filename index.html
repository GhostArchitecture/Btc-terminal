Aggregation. Spot = median of sources fresher than 20 s. Sources polled every 5 s (CoinGecko 60 s; it is itself a composite index). Binance.com excluded: HTTP 451 geo-block for US clients.

Seeding. Return statistics are initialized from Kraken 1-minute OHLC (last ~180 bars), then rolled forward from live 1-minute bars of the median.

Models. Volatility: EWMA (RiskMetrics, λ = 0.94) on 1-min log returns. Martingale: E[P] = spot; 90% band = ±1.645·σ√h. GBM: 1,500 Monte-Carlo paths, drift = mean of last 120 one-min returns; cone = per-step percentiles. OU: AR(1) on log-price deviation from a 30-min mean; deviation decays as bʰ; suppressed when no reversion is detected (b ≥ 0.999). Momentum: EWMA drift (10-min half-life) extrapolated, clamped to ±2.5·σ√h. Ensemble: median of available point forecasts.

Interval discipline. Every minute one forecast set is pinned to five wall-clock due times. Realized price at each due instant is linearly interpolated from the tick series (never a late sample; residual resolution lag is reported in the scorecard header). Accuracy is scored per interval — the five horizons are never blended. Sets are spaced 60 s apart so consecutive resolutions are not pseudo-replicated autocorrelated samples; the matrix above refreshes faster (≤20 s) for display, but accountability runs on the pinned sets.

Chart. Wicks are 1-minute OHLC of the consensus median (green up, red down); the y-axis keeps current price at vertical center with the band spreading symmetrically. The projection cone is pinned to its minute's set — it does not re-base with the display — so as time passes the candles advance into it and paint over the elapsed region: agreement is visible where wicks stay inside amber. Due-lines drift toward the NOW divider and brighten in their final 30 s; on crossing, a verdict node is minted — ring at the projection, dot at the realized price, ring green when the print landed inside that set's 90% band. The running band-coverage figure against its 90% target is the calibration verdict. Honest framing: point-forecast confidence is capped by the martingale property; what can be demonstrated to resolve accurately is the dispersion, and that is what the nodes audit.

Benchmark. The martingale MAE equals the realized move magnitude — the floor every other model must beat. At 5–60 min horizons BTC log-price is empirically near-martingale: expected drift is ~1–3 bp against a typical σ of 30–100 bp, so point forecasts cluster at spot and direction hit rates oscillate around 50%. The informative output is the dispersion (cone), not the direction. Nothing here is trading advice; the scorecard exists to measure these models against live data rather than assert their skill.
